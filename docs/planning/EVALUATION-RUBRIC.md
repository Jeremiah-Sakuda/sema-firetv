# Sema PRD v2.0 — evaluator instructions

Review PRD-v2.0.md as an independent evaluator. This is an AI-assisted pre-build panel, not actual Amazon judging, target-user research, or a prediction of placement. Do not treat planned requirements as implemented evidence. Evaluate the document as frozen; do not edit it.

## Official rubric

Source: https://amazonappdev2026.devpost.com/rules and the user's supplied rules.

- **Stage One:** pass/fail baseline viability, fit to theme, and reasonable use of required track technology. Fire TV requires a working Fire OS/Vega app shown on actual Fire TV or a qualifying simulator. A PRD can only be conditionally eligible.
- **Tech Implementation (25%):** how well the project is built and how effectively it uses required APIs, SDKs, or device capabilities.
- **Design (25%):** complete, coherent experience; intuitive and well-considered interaction model for the target device/platform.
- **Potential Impact (25%):** credible, specific customer need and realistic audience beyond the hackathon.
- **Quality of the Idea (25%):** creative use of required tools, understanding of developer ecosystem and end-user needs. Basic players are obvious; adaptive AI viewing and coherent multi-modal interaction are illustrative creative examples, not mandatory feature checklists.
- **Friction bonus:** up to 10%, assessed by organizers from actual actionable logs. Award no bonus for a planned log.
- **Tie break:** criterion order above, then panel vote if still tied.

## Panel scoring convention (not an official Devpost scale)

Use 0–10 for each criterion to score **the quality and credibility of the proposed plan**, not completed product performance. Equal-weight composite = arithmetic mean of four scores. Anchors: 0–2 absent/incoherent; 3–4 major unresolved gaps; 5–6 plausible but substantial weaknesses; 7–8 strong plan with identifiable risks; 9 exceptional specificity/feasibility; 10 no material planning gap found. Do not issue a separate hypothetical “implemented perfectly” score or win probability.

For every criterion give score, PRD requirement/section references, rationale, and missing evidence. Give a conditional Stage One assessment. Identify up to five highest-impact concerns with severity (P0 feasibility/eligibility blocker, P1 material scoring or user-experience risk, P2 improvement), a concrete suggested change, and how to verify it. Distinguish an internal spec defect from a sensible requirement whose implementation is simply unproven.

Conclude with: strongest differentiator; strongest skeptical judge objection; build/conditional-build/rework recommendation; three next experiments. Be candid. Do not manufacture criticism for equal coverage and do not claim lived experience or actual judge authority.
