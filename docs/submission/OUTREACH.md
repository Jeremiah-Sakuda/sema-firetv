- Local filmmakers

**Subject:** Can Sema add audio description to your 1-minute short? (credited, non-exclusive, free)

Hi [name],

I'm building **Sema**, a Fire TV app that adds human-reviewed audio description (AD) to short films. Blind and low-vision viewers can choose how much detail they hear, and ask "What did I miss?" whenever they need to. Sema uses AWS AI services to draft the description, and a person reviews every line before it's published.

I'd love to feature a **45–60 second excerpt of your film** in our Amazon Developer Hackathon entry (deadline October 23):

- **Credit:** you're credited by name in the app, the README, and the demo video.

- **Your time:** a 15-minute walkthrough of the reviewed description. Your feedback ("would you publish this?") is what I'm hoping to learn.

If you're open to it, I'll send a one-paragraph permission note for you to sign.

Thanks,
